## Prompt 1: Inversion Problem Solving

### Prompt
```
Use reverse thinking to solve this problem:
"How do we increase user retention for our SaaS product?"

Step 1: Invert the question - "How would we GUARANTEE users leave?"
Step 2: List all ways to drive users away
Step 3: Flip each answer to find retention strategies
Step 4: Prioritize the flipped strategies by impact

Format as a two-column comparison table.
```
