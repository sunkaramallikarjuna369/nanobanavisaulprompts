## Prompt 1: SCAMPER Method

### Prompt
```
Use the SCAMPER brainstorming method to generate innovative ideas
for improving a mobile banking app.

Apply each SCAMPER lens:
S - Substitute: What can be replaced?
C - Combine: What can be merged together?
A - Adapt: What can be adapted from other industries?
M - Modify: What can be enlarged, shrunk, or changed?
P - Put to other use: What else can it be used for?
E - Eliminate: What can be removed?
R - Reverse: What can be flipped or reordered?

For each lens, provide 2 concrete ideas with feasibility rating.
```
