# Science to Illustrations Prompts

Transform scientific content into detailed visual illustrations.

---

## 71. Cellular Diagram

```
Transform this biology process into a detailed cellular diagram with organelles labeled, zoomed inset views, and color-coded functions.
```

**Example:** Input: Cell respiration text → Output: Cell diagram with mitochondria zoomed, ATP molecules, glucose → energy arrows

---

## 72. Molecular Visualization

```
Convert this chemistry reaction into a molecular visualization with atoms as colored spheres, bonds as sticks, before/after states.
```

**Example:** Input: Chemical equation → Output: Left: H2O molecules, Right: H2 + O2, color-coded atoms, broken/formed bonds shown

---

## 73. Force Diagram

```
Turn this physics concept into a force diagram with objects, force vectors as arrows, angle measurements, and result motion.
```

**Example:** Input: Newton's laws text → Output: Box on incline, gravity arrow down, normal force up, friction left, motion vectors

## 74. Astronomy Diagram
