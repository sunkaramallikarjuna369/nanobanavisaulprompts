# Math to Visual Proofs Prompts

Transform mathematical concepts into visual demonstrations and diagrams.

---

## 81. Visual Proof

```
Transform this mathematical proof into a visual demonstration with geometric shapes, algebraic steps, and color-coded equivalences.
```

**Example:** Input: Pythagorean theorem proof → Output: Right triangle with squares on each side, area calculations shown, a²+b²=c² highlighted

---

## 82. Calculus Diagram

